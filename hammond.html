Do It Yourself or Hire a Pro

Hiring a Pro vs Painting It Yourself in Hammond

A DIY paint job can turn into a lot more work than expected. Here is what tends to happen, and what a professional crew handles for you.

Where DIY Goes Wrong

  • Painting in heat that makes paint peel or crack early
  • Rain arriving before the paint has dried
  • Flaky old paint left under the new coat
  • Cracks and holes painted over, not filled
  • Long days on tall ladders

What a Pro Brings

  • Exterior work timed for milder, drier weather
  • Walls cleaned and sanded before painting
  • Cracks and holes filled with caulk or joint compound
  • High-quality materials that hold up for years
  • A crew that handles the ladders and the heights
All About Hammond

Hammond, in Full Color

Hammond is the largest city in Tangipahoa Parish, on Louisiana's Northshore where Interstates 12 and 55 meet, between New Orleans and Baton Rouge.

  • ParishTangipahoa

Things to Do in Hammond

  1. Exhibit 01

    Columbia Theatre for the Performing Arts

    A downtown theater that opened in 1928 and is now run by Southeastern Louisiana University, hosting plays, musicals, films, and concerts.

  2. Exhibit 02

    Joyce Wildlife Management Area Swamp Walk

    A state-run boardwalk south of Hammond that leads into a cypress-tupelo swamp for viewing wildlife.

When Is the Best Time to Paint a House Exterior in Hammond?

Late spring and early fall usually work best, when temperatures are mild and the air is less humid. Summer heat speeds up drying, which can lead to peeling or cracking, and we plan around rain so paint has time to dry.

What Exterior Colors Hold Up Well in Hammond?

In a warm climate, lighter colors like soft blues and greens reflect more heat. Look at what complements the houses around you, and pick paint made for the local weather.

How Should I Prepare My Hammond Home Before Painting?

Walls get cleaned with an all-purpose or degreasing cleaner, flaky paint gets sanded off, and cracks and holes get filled. We take care of this prep as part of the job.

Do You Do Repairs Along With Painting?

Yes. We handle minor repairs and drywall repair before painting, plus staining, refinishing, and power washing.

What Affects the Cost of Painting a Home in Hammond?

The condition of the building, the size of the project, and any extra work needed beyond painting. We look at all of it during your free estimate.

Do You Paint Businesses in Hammond Too?

Yes. We paint both homes and businesses in Hammond and the surrounding area.

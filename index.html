How Can You Tell if a Painter Is Good in New Orleans?

Ask whether they're licensed and insured, how they handle prep, and whether they'll share references. Big Easy Painting is licensed and insured, walks you through the plan before work starts, and provides references on request.

How Humid Is Too Humid to Paint?

It depends on the product. Every paint lists its own limits for temperature, humidity, and dew point on the label. Painting outside those limits can cause slow drying, poor adhesion, and blistering, so on exterior jobs the label sets the rules for the day.

What Is the Best Time of Year to Paint in New Orleans?

Interior painting works any time of year. Exteriors do best during drier, milder stretches, which is why fall and spring are popular. Your estimate is a good time to talk through timing for your project.

Is It Better to Roll or Spray Exterior Paint?

Both have a place. Spraying covers large, flat areas quickly. Brushing and rolling work paint into rough or porous surfaces like older wood and brick. Many exterior jobs use a mix, and we'll recommend what suits your home.

Is It Worth Having Kitchen Cabinets Professionally Painted?

For many homeowners, yes. Cabinets take daily wear, so a lasting finish depends on careful cleaning, sanding, and priming. A professional job gives you an even, durable finish without replacing the cabinets.

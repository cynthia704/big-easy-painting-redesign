Revamp Your Home’s Exterior: 10 Creative Ideas for Updating With Paint in New Orleans, LA

When it comes to updating the exterior of your home, a fresh coat of paint can make a big impact. Not only does it improve curb appeal, but it also protects your home from the elements. If you’re looking for some inspiration to update your home’s exterior with paint, look no further! In this blog post, we’ll share some ideas from Big Easy Painting to help you get started.

Choose a Bold Color

One of the easiest ways to update the exterior of your home is to choose a bold color for your front door. This is a great way to add personality and make a statement. You can also consider painting your shutters or trim in a contrasting color to add some visual interest.

If you’re not ready to commit to a bold color for your entire front door, you can also consider painting just the trim around the door or the door frame. This is a great way to add a pop of color without going too overboard.

Embrace Neutrals

If you prefer a more subdued look, neutrals are always a safe bet. They provide a classic, timeless look and are a great way to update the exterior of your home without being too trendy. You can’t go wrong with colors like beige, gray, or white.

To add some interest to a neutral color palette, consider painting your front door in a bold color. This will create a nice contrast and make your front entry stand out.

Use Accents

Another way to update your home’s exterior is to use accent colors. These are colors that are used sparingly to create visual interest. For example, you can paint your window trim, shutters, or front porch railing in an accent color.

When choosing an accent color, make sure it complements the main color of your home’s exterior. You can choose a complementary color on the color wheel or use a color that is already present in your landscaping or other exterior features.

Experiment With Textures

Paint isn’t just for flat surfaces. You can also use paint to create interesting textures on your home’s exterior. For example, you can use textured paint to add some depth and dimension to your front door. You can also use a stencil or painter’s tape to create a geometric pattern on your front porch.

Textured paint can also be used to hide imperfections on your home’s exterior. If you have a wall with cracks or bumps, textured paint can help to mask these issues.

Consider Your Home’s Architecture

When choosing a color palette for your home’s exterior, it’s important to consider the architecture of your home. Certain colors and styles will look better on certain types of homes.

For example, a traditional colonial-style home will look great in classic colors like white, beige, or navy blue. A modern home, on the other hand, may look better in bold, bright colors like orange or turquoise.

Don’t Forget About the Garage

Many homeowners forget about the garage when updating their home’s exterior. However, the garage door can be a major focal point and impact the overall look of your home. If your garage door is looking a little worn or outdated, consider giving it a fresh coat of paint.

You can choose to match the color of your front door or use an accent color to make it stand out. If you have a wood garage door, you can also consider staining it for a natural look.

Mix and Match

Who says you have to choose just one color for your home’s exterior? Mixing and matching colors can create a unique and eye-catching look. For example, you can choose a main color for your home’s siding and then use a contrasting color for your front door, shutters, or window trim.

When mixing and matching colors, it’s important to choose colors that complement each other. A good rule of thumb is to choose colors that are either complementary or analogous on the color wheel.

Consider the Season

If you live in an area with distinct seasons, you can use paint to update your home’s exterior throughout the year. For example, in the spring and summer, you can choose light, bright colors that reflect the season. In the fall and winter, you can choose warmer, more subdued colors.

You can also use paint to decorate your home for the holidays. For example, you can paint your front door red or green for Christmas or use pastel colors for Easter.

Get Creative With Paint Finishes

When most people think of exterior paint, they think of a matte or glossy finish. However, there are a variety of paint finishes that can be used to create different looks and textures. For example, you can use a metallic finish for your front door or use a chalky finish for your shutters.

Paint finishes can also be used to create a distressed or aged look. This is a great way to add some character and charm to your home’s exterior.

Hire a Professional

Painting the exterior of your home can be a big project, and it’s not something that should be taken lightly. If you’re not comfortable with DIY or if you want to ensure that the job is done right, consider hiring a professional painting company like Big Easy Painting.

A professional painter will have the experience and expertise to properly prep your home’s exterior, choose the right paint, and apply it evenly. They can also offer advice and ideas to help you achieve the look you want.

Conclusion

Updating the exterior of your home with paint is a great way to improve curb appeal, protect your home from the elements, and express your personal style. With these ideas from Big Easy Painting, you can get started on your home’s exterior makeover today.
